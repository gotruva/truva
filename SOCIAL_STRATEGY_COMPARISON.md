# Social Strategy: Gemini v1 vs Corrected v2 — Comparison
**Date:** June 4, 2026 | **Purpose:** Show exactly what changed between the original Gemini draft and the corrected, brand-aligned version, and why.

> **TL;DR:** v1 had strong bones (right channels, right pillars, smart AI pipeline) but three trust-breaking errors and several brand mismatches. v2 keeps the bones and fixes the trust layer. For a brand whose entire promise is *"we tell you the truth banks hide,"* the fixes aren't cosmetic — they're existential.

---

## 1. Side-by-side

| Dimension | Gemini v1 (original) | Corrected v2 | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **After-tax framing** | "Calculate Your True After-Tax Yield" as a primary bio link; Reddit play built on after-tax math | All after-tax language removed; calculator labeled "advertised/gross estimates"; Reddit play explains mechanics, not tax math | Breaks Non-Negotiable Rule #3 (no after-tax shown to users). v1 also mislabeled the live `/calculator`, which is gross-only |
| **PDIC Split Calculator (Reddit sign-off)** | Recommends "the free PDIC Split Calculator on Truva" | Reference removed until the tool is live | `/optimizer` currently redirects to the homepage — v1 sent high-intent users to a tool that doesn't exist |
| **Visual direction** | Dark-mode dominant (`#0A0B0D` backgrounds); claimed it "matches the website" | Light/white-first (`#FFFFFF`), matching the actual site; all image prompts rewritten | The site is white-first; `#0A0B0D` is the *text* color. v1's "matches website" claim was false |
| **"50+ credit cards" claim** | Stated as fact in both example posts | Softened to "compare side by side" until catalog supports a number | Phase 2 cards are an active build; a hard number you can't back erodes trust |
| **UnionBank "catch"** | Asserts "must spend ₱20,000 in 60 days to waive fee" as the headline catch | **Now verified TRUE** (Moneymax / DTI Permit FTEB-212281 s.2026, checked 2026-06-04). Post built on the real catch: "free for life *if* you spend ₱20,000 in 60 days" | Plot twist: Gemini v1 was actually *right* here. A mid-draft of v2 wrongly assumed the condition was outdated — the expert review caught it. This is the exact reason the Fact-Check Protocol exists: verify before assuming, in *either* direction |
| **Card numbers in posts** | Presented as final/"Ready-to-Post" | Every fee/rate/income tagged `[VERIFY]` with source+date line | LLMs invent finance numbers; a wrong figure in a comparison is doubly damaging |
| **Fact-check process** | None — model output treated as truth | Mandatory Fact-Check Protocol + pre-post checklist; Truva data is source of truth, LLM only reformats | The single biggest risk in AI-made finance content |
| **Affiliate disclosure** | "This doesn't change your rate" (odd for cards); exclamation-heavy | Master wording: "does not change what you are offered"; calm on data | Consistency + accuracy; "rate" is wrong framing for a credit card |
| **Voice** | Emoji/exclamation throughout, including on data | Fun in hooks, calm/exact on numbers; one emoji per heading; no Taglish in graphics | Keeps content lively but protects the trusted-guide tone |
| **Audience** | Narrowed to "Millennials/Gen-Z 24–36, cashback/miles optimizers" | Keeps CC focus for Phase 2 but restores the wider brand audience incl. first-timers/unbanked | v1 quietly dropped Persona C (~50% of PH is unbanked) and bumped the age range |

---

## 2. What v1 got RIGHT (kept in v2)
- **Channel choice:** Instagram + TikTok Photo Mode + Facebook is the correct PH-market mix.
- **Content pillars:** "What's the Catch," Card Battles, "Free for Life," and a savings/rate support pillar — all high-intent and on-brand.
- **AI co-op pipeline:** Gemini (analyst) → Claude (editor) → ChatGPT/DALL·E (designer) → packer is a smart solo-founder workflow. v2 keeps it and adds a verification gate.
- **Three-question structure** ("What is it / Who is it for / What's the catch") mirrors Truva's product pages — good consistency.
- **Defensive Reddit approach** (unbranded, helpful) — right instinct; v2 just removes the fake-tool reference and adds "verify the community exists."
- **Brand color tokens and fonts** were already correct (#0052FF, #12B76A, #F79009, Space Grotesk/Inter).

---

## 3. Net assessment
v1 was a solid *first draft from a model that didn't have your guardrails loaded.* The strategy direction is sound; the failures were all in **accuracy and trust** — exactly the layer Truva cannot compromise. v2 is safe to execute **once each card's numbers are verified** against the bank's live pages (see the Fact-Check Protocol in the corrected playbook). The remaining open question isn't strategy — it's building a repeatable system so verification and production scale without burning out a solo founder. That system is covered in the Content Operating System doc.
